About Me

Stefanos Voikos is a Research Assistant at the Network Management and Optimal Design Laboratory at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA).

Following my MSc, I have transitioned into doctoral studies focusing on the intersection of Next-Generation Wireless Networks (6G) and Distributed Intelligence. My current work explores the orchestration of radio and compute resources to support massive-scale Federated Learning, being passionate about applying optimization and distributed learning techniques to enhance the scalability and energy efficiency of next-generation networks.
